The medical workforce, in six years

HSE publishes a dense, ~100-page Medical Workforce Report every year — full of real, specific numbers on doctor supply, vacancies, and who's filling the gaps. Almost nobody reads the whole thing. This pulls six years of it into one page.

ⓘ Scope note: 2019/20–2024/25 only — the years the report consistently covers both NCHDs and Consultants (it was NCHD-only before, under a different title, back to 2010). HSE's own site was down when this was pulled; every figure here traces to a specific table/figure in a specific year's PDF — see data/raw/hse/SOURCES.md for exact citations, including one real data revision worth knowing about.
